# Privacy Policy — Kosmos Cut Pro

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026  
**Developer:** Egehan Bakıcı (Turkey)  
**App:** Kosmos Cut Pro (iOS)

This Privacy Policy explains what information Kosmos Cut Pro (“the App,” “we,” “us”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain English so you can understand it quickly.

**Short version:** Video editing happens on your device. We do not show ads, we do not track you across apps or websites for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data. Some optional features (Sign in with Apple, cloud AI tools, and review links) only run after you choose to use them and, where required, after you give consent.

---

## 1. Who we are

Kosmos Cut Pro is developed by **Egehan Bakıcı**, based in Turkey. For privacy questions or requests under GDPR or KVKK (Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law), contact us through the support channel listed on the App’s App Store page or inside the App.

---

## 2. What this App does with your content

### On-device editing (default)

Core video editing runs **on your device**. Media you import or create for editing stays on your device unless you intentionally use a feature that uploads it (described below).

### Optional features that involve upload

Only if you turn them on or use them:

| Feature | What leaves your device | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sign in with Apple** | Account identifiers Apple provides to us | When you choose to sign in |
| **Cloud AI features** (generation, dubbing, text-to-speech, voice cloning) | Video and/or audio you select for that job | Only after you consent and start the feature |
| **Review links** | Exported videos you choose to share via a review link | When you create or share a review link |
| **Purchases** | Purchase/subscription status via Apple and RevenueCat | When you buy or restore |
| **Crash reporting** | Technical crash diagnostics via Sentry | When the App crashes or reports an error |

We do **not** use your content for advertising. We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 3. Information we collect and why

### A. Information you provide or generate

- **Media for cloud AI (optional, consent-gated):** Video and/or audio you upload so we can run the AI feature you requested (for example generation, dubbing, text-to-speech, or voice cloning). Purpose: deliver that feature. We process this only after you give consent and start the job.
- **Exported videos for review links (optional):** Videos you choose to upload so others can review them via a link. Purpose: provide sharing/review.
- **Account (optional):** If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the identifiers and any name/email Apple shares with us under your Apple settings. Purpose: authenticate you, sync account-related features, and respond to account or deletion requests.

### B. Purchase information

- We use **Apple** (In-App Purchase / StoreKit) and **RevenueCat** to process and validate subscriptions and purchases. We may receive purchase status, product identifiers, and anonymous or pseudonymous customer identifiers needed to unlock paid features and restore purchases. Purpose: fulfill purchases, prevent fraud, and provide customer support for billing.

We do not receive your full payment card number; Apple handles payment.

### C. Diagnostics and crash data

- We use **Sentry** to collect crash reports and related technical diagnostics (for example device type, OS version, App version, stack traces, and similar technical logs). Purpose: find and fix bugs and keep the App stable.

We configure crash reporting for product reliability, not for advertising or cross-app tracking.

### D. What we do not collect for ads or sale

- No third-party advertising networks.
- No sale of personal data.
- No tracking of you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising purposes.

## 4. Legal bases (GDPR / similar laws)

Where GDPR (or similar laws) applies, we rely on:

- **Contract / service delivery** — to provide the App features you use (including purchases you make).
- **Consent** — for optional cloud AI processing of your video/audio (and any other processing that requires consent under applicable law). You can withdraw consent by stopping use of those features and, where available, using in-App controls; withdrawing consent does not affect processing already completed lawfully.
- **Legitimate interests** — security, fraud prevention, and crash diagnostics, balanced against your rights.
- **Legal obligation** — when we must keep or disclose information to comply with law.

Under **KVKK**, we process personal data in line with Turkish law, including for providing the service you request, fulfilling contracts (such as purchases), and, where required, based on your explicit consent for sensitive or optional processing such as cloud AI on your media.

## 6. How long we keep data

- **On-device media:** Controlled by you on your device; we do not keep a copy unless you upload it through an optional feature.
- **Cloud AI uploads:** Kept only as long as needed to complete the job you requested, provide the result, and handle abuse, security, or support — then deleted or anonymized according to our retention schedule. If you ask us to delete earlier, we will do so unless we must retain something briefly for legal or security reasons.
- **Review-link videos:** Kept while the link is active or until you delete it / request deletion, then removed from our systems within a reasonable period.
- **Account data (Sign in with Apple):** Kept while your account is active. If you request deletion, we delete or anonymize account data except where we must retain limited records (for example purchase history required for accounting or fraud prevention).
- **Purchase records:** Kept as needed for accounting, tax, chargebacks, and App Store rules.
- **Crash reports (Sentry):** Kept for a limited period needed to debug and improve the App, then deleted or aggregated.

Exact periods may vary with technical and legal requirements; we aim to keep personal data no longer than necessary.
