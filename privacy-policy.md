# Privacy Policy — Kosmos Cut Pro

**Effective date:** October 8, 2026  
**Developer:** Egehan Bakıcı (Turkey)  
**Contact:** [ege.bakici35@gmail.com](mailto:ege.bakici35@gmail.com) · [Support](support.html)

**Short version:** Editing happens on your iPhone. We never receive your videos, photos, or audio. We don't show ads, track you, or sell data. The only data the App itself sends is your purchase and subscription information, which goes to Apple and RevenueCat when the App starts and when you buy or restore.

## 1. Your media
Everything you import, edit, and export stays on your iPhone. That includes the on-device AI features: captions, tracking, reframe, background removal, and beat sync. We never receive your videos, photos, or audio.

The App asks for access to your Photos (to import media and to save exports), and to your camera and microphone (to record voiceovers and teleprompter takes). Everything they capture stays on your iPhone. A video leaves your iPhone only when you send it somewhere yourself, for example through the share sheet or iCloud Photos.

The first time you use captions or translation, iOS may download Apple's speech or language models to your iPhone. None of your media is sent to do this.

## 2. Purchases
Subscriptions (Pro Weekly, Pro Yearly) are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives:
- an anonymous app user ID;
- the products you bought and their transaction dates, from Apple;
- the technical details any internet request carries, such as App and iOS version, store country, and IP address.

It does not receive your name, email, or payment card. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 3. Crash reports
If you allow "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports (device model, iOS version, App version). They contain no media.

## 4. What we don't collect
No account, name, email, phone, contacts, location, advertising identifier, or browsing history.

## 5. Retention and deletion
- **Media and projects:** on your device until you delete them or the App.
- **Purchase records at RevenueCat:** kept while needed for your subscription and for tax and accounting obligations.
- **Support emails:** kept only as long as needed to help you.
- **To ask us to delete anything we hold:** email ege.bakici35@gmail.com.

## 6. Your rights (GDPR, KVKK, CCPA)
You can ask to access, correct, delete, or port your data, or object to processing. Email ege.bakici35@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

There is no processing that relies on your consent for you to withdraw, because we don't collect your media. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey, the KVKK Board).

## 7. Third parties
Apple and RevenueCat process data only to provide their services. They are bound to protect it at least as strongly as this policy does.

## 8. Children
The App is not directed at children under 13.

## 9. Changes
We'll update the effective date. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the App.
