## Frequently asked questions


### How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with, open the App, and tap **Restore Purchases**. It's on the subscription screen and in Account.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open **Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions**, choose Kosmos Cut Pro, and tap **Cancel Subscription**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next renewal.

### Where are my projects saved?

On your iPhone, as you edit. Deleting the App deletes its projects, so export finished videos to Photos before you remove it.
